# SIH26124
An edge-AI platform that turns existing bus-mounted cameras into real-time urban intelligence sensors — detecting road defects, traffic incidents, and safety hazards city-wide, with no new infrastructure.

Let me start with the problem. Buses already travel every road, every day — with cameras on board. But those cameras just record; they don't think.
At the same time, cities are making maintenance and safety decisions using data that's weeks old. A pothole takes nearly 25 days from being noticed to getting a repair order. In that window, vehicles are damaged and accidents happen.
This affects everyone — 600 million urban commuters, traffic police with no plate evidence in hit-and-run cases, school children crossing unmonitored roads, and city engineers planning maintenance without real data.
The cameras are already there. The buses are already running. The only missing piece is intelligence."
